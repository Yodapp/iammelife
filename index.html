I AM ME · A LIFE STILL UNFOLDING

Sophie Thomasdotter · In her own words

Notes from a life
being rewritten.

01 · Love that remains

Some relationships don’t end because we choose to leave.

They end because life chooses for us. One day, someone is there. The next, they’re not. We never get to say: “I’m not finished yet.”

I don’t believe time heals every wound. I believe we learn to breathe around the pain, building a life where grief is allowed to exist without taking up all the space.

Love doesn’t die when a person does. It only changes form.
02 · A letter to herself

You didn’t stay because you were weak.

You stayed because your heart has always believed in the good in people. But loving someone’s potential can slowly make you abandon who you already are.

Love should never require you to give up your safety, your self-respect or your sense of worth. The day you have to fight to be chosen is the day you already have your answer.

Today, I choose myself.
03 · The voice beneath the noise

Sometimes it’s not the darkness that makes us lose our way.

It’s the noise — every feeling speaking at once, every memory asking to be heard, every fear shouting louder than hope can whisper.

Truth rarely raises its voice. It waits. And when the storm begins to settle, it returns as a quiet reminder.

You knew the way all along. You only needed to find your way back to your own voice.
04 · Her unfinished house

No expectations. Only hope, determination and the will to continue.

My house is still far from finished, and sometimes the nails still go in crooked. I’m still a student of life, even when my experiences allow me to guide someone else.

I wrote a book to help others reflect on their own lives. I write music in the hope that people will pause and truly listen. Not to make myself a victim — but to show that change is possible.

To begin again. Not from zero, but from a new angle.

“We don’t need to know the whole road. We only need to find the courage to begin walking.”

With all my love · Sophie

The memoir

Some stories refuse to stay silent.

I Am Me

How much of yourself can you lose before you no longer recognize who you are?

Discover the memoir on Amazon ↗

Caught in a cycle of emotional abuse, manipulation, violence and self-destruction, she slowly lost sight of the person she once was. As a mother, she fought to protect her children while struggling to survive herself.

When a massive stroke forced everything to stop, it became impossible to ignore the truths she had spent a lifetime avoiding.

I Am Me is a raw and deeply personal memoir about finding the strength to reclaim your voice, your worth and your identity.

“I didn’t write to make myself a victim. I wrote to show that change is possible.”
